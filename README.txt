PRIYANK & AMITA — SIMPLE PERSONALIZED WEDDING INVITATION + QR GENERATOR

Files:
- index.html: the animated wedding invitation and personalized guest welcome popup.
- qr-generator.html: a browser-based QR generator for individual guests or many guests at once.

NO REACT, NODE.JS, API, OR DATABASE REQUIRED.

HOW TO USE:
1. Upload index.html to a public static website host.
2. Open qr-generator.html in a browser (it can also be uploaded to your site).
3. Enter the public URL where index.html is hosted, e.g. https://yourdomain.com/.
4. Enter guest names, one per line, and click Generate QR Codes.
5. Download each guest's QR as a PNG, or click Print All QR Codes to print a sheet.
6. When a guest scans their QR, the link includes ?guest=Guest%20Name. The invitation shows one welcome popup with that name. Tapping the button opens the invitation.

NOTES:
- QR generation uses QRCode.js from cdnjs, so internet access is required when opening qr-generator.html.
- Guest names are encoded in the URL query string. Anyone with the URL can see that name; do not put sensitive information there.
- A public URL is required for guests to access the invitation. Local file URLs will not work for other people.
